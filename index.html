Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours and reach most cities in 3–5 working days via trusted couriers.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes — COD is available all over Pakistan. You pay in cash when the parcel arrives at your doorstep.

What if I want to exchange?

We offer a 7-day easy exchange for unused sets in original packing — for size, colour or manufacturing issues.

Yes! Boutiques, resellers and event planners get special wholesale pricing on 5+ sets. Message us on WhatsApp for the wholesale catalogue.
